# Krema Brand Kit

Krema's identity is built from one idea: **the dock zooms**. The app icon draws a
"K" out of a dock, and the wordmark sets its letters the way the dock sizes
icons under the pointer. Everything promotional lives under `branding/` — this
is the single promo kit.

## Layout

| Directory | Contents |
|-----------|----------|
| `logo/` | SVG masters: icon, small icon, wordmark, horizontal and stacked lockups (light + dark). Regenerated by `logo/generate.ts`. |
| `palette/` | `palette.svg` swatch sheet + `palette.json` / `palette.css` tokens (Palette A, shared with `website/css/site.css`). Regenerated by `palette/generate.ts`. |
| `social/` | Social/store cards: one SVG master + one PNG export per asset. Regenerated by `social/generate.ts`. |
| `screenshots/` | Real Plasma desktop screenshots (1920×1080). Regeneration pipeline in `screenshots/regen/`, current captures via `clips/stills.sh` — see `screenshots/NOTES.md`. |
| `wallpaper/` | "Roast Contours" wallpaper used in screenshots and clips + its generator. |
| `clips/` | Headless-Plasma capture scripts that produce the clips and stills served from `website/media/`. See `clips/NOTES.md`. |
| `panel-ref/` | Pixel-reference captures of the real Plasma panel/Breeze widgets used to build the site's panel demo. Not brand art — keep as-is. |

Every committed SVG is self-contained: all text is baked to paths (usvg + the
pinned Noto Sans file), so assets render identically on any machine without the
font installed. Logo masters contain no text at all.

## Concept

### Zoom K (app icon)

- **Stem** (bone) is the dock panel.
- **Arms** are caramel rounded tiles (dock icons) that shrink and fade as
  they move away from the largest tile next to the stem. The falloff follows the
  same parabolic zoom curve the dock uses on hover.
- The ink tile behind it keeps the mark legible on both light and dark desktops.

### Zoom wordmark

Hand-drawn monoline lowercase `krema`. Each letter is scaled like an icon in a
zooming dock, with the pointer resting on the `e`:

| Letter | k | r | e | m | a |
|--------|------|------|------|------|------|
| Scale  | 0.72 | 0.87 | 1.16 | 0.87 | 0.72 |

The caramel dot under the `e` is the dock's active-indicator. It is always
the accent color, whichever ink the letters use. Never re-scale letters
individually, even out the sizes, or move the dot to another letter.

The icon has a dark tile of its own, so it works on any background. Only the
wordmark and lockups have light/dark variants; pick the one whose ink contrasts
with the surface behind it. On the web, switch between them with
`<picture>` and `prefers-color-scheme` (see the top of the project README).

## Palette

Tokens live in `palette/` (`palette.svg`, `palette.json`, `palette.css`) and
mirror `website/css/site.css`.

| Swatch | Name | Hex | Role |
|--------|------|-----|------|
| ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%20%20%20%20-18110D?style=flat-square) | Ink | `#18110D` | Icon tile, wordmark ink on light backgrounds, dark surfaces |
| ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%20%20%20%20-F4ECE0?style=flat-square) | Bone | `#F4ECE0` | Icon stem (the dock panel), wordmark ink on dark backgrounds, light surfaces |
| ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%20%20%20%20-D69A5E?style=flat-square) | Caramel | `#D69A5E` | Accent only: icon tiles, the indicator dot, links and highlights |

Secondary colors for layouts (never inside the logo): `#110C09` (night, the
deepest background), `#231A14` (ink-2, raised dark surface), `#CDBFAE`
(bone-dim, body copy on dark), `#A89684` (muted, secondary copy), `#A8682F`
(caramel-deep, pressed or hover accent).

Caramel is an accent. Use it for small, meaningful marks; do not flood large
areas with it.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the icon equal to at least ¼ of the
  icon's width, and around lockups equal to at least the height of the
  wordmark's `e`. No text, borders, or other logos inside that area.
- **Icon:** 16 px minimum. From 16 to 24 px use `krema-icon-small.svg`; at 32 px
  and above use `krema-icon.svg`. The full icon's smallest tiles disappear
  below 32 px.
- **Wordmark:** at least 80 px wide on screen. Below that, use the icon alone.
- **Horizontal lockup:** at least 48 px tall (the README uses 96 px). Below
  that, use the icon alone.

## Don'ts

- Don't recolor the tiles, the stem, or the indicator dot. The tiles are always
  Caramel, the stem is always Bone, the tile is always Ink.
- Don't rotate, skew, mirror, or add shadows, gradients, or outlines to any mark.
- Don't reorder, respace, or re-scale the wordmark letters, or typeset "krema"
  in another font as a stand-in for the wordmark.
- Don't use a dark-ink wordmark on a dark background or a light-ink one on a
  light background.
- Don't redraw the icon's arms with a different number or size of tiles; the
  shrinking sequence is the zoom falloff.
- Don't place the Krema logo next to the Latte Dock logo or the KDE logo in a way
  that implies affiliation or endorsement. Krema is an independent project; its
  only relation to Latte Dock is being its spiritual successor.

## Regenerating

Everything is generated; edit the generator, not the outputs. From the
repository root:

```sh
bun branding/logo/generate.ts      # logo masters into branding/logo/
bun branding/social/generate.ts    # social SVG masters + PNG exports
bun branding/palette/generate.ts   # palette.svg + palette.json + palette.css
```

The social and palette generators need `nix` (they use `usvg` to bake text to
paths with a pinned Noto Sans, `resvg` to rasterize, `oxipng` to optimize).
`--svg-only` on `social/generate.ts` skips the PNG step.

Rasterize masters manually when needed:

```sh
# Icon at a given pixel size (use the small variant for 16/22/24 px)
nix shell nixpkgs#resvg -c resvg -w 256 branding/logo/krema-icon.svg krema-256.png
nix shell nixpkgs#resvg -c resvg -w 22 branding/logo/krema-icon-small.svg krema-22.png

# Lockup at a fixed height (nested SVGs resolve relative to --resources-dir)
nix shell nixpkgs#resvg -c resvg -h 192 --resources-dir branding/logo \
  branding/logo/krema-lockup.svg krema-lockup.png

# Optimize every PNG before committing
nix shell nixpkgs#oxipng -c oxipng -o 4 --strip safe krema-256.png
```

Deploy copies (`src/icons/`, `website/favicon*`, `website/icon-*.png`,
`apple-touch-icon.png`, `maskable-512.png`) are rendered from these masters per
the mapping table above — regenerate them from the kit when the logo changes;
never edit them directly.
