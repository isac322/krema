# Krema: Marketing & Positioning Mandate

> **Identity:** Krema is a "spiritual successor" to Latte Dock. Never a "fork," "clone," or "rewrite."

## 1. Value Proposition (Why users want Krema)
- **The 3-Tier "Tree of Islands":** A modular revolution. Your dock is no longer a static bar, but a recursive hierarchy of Panels, Islands, and Items, enabling unmatched layout flexibility.
- **Tablet-Class Recursive Folders:** Organize your workflow with nested folders that expand inline or via pop-up grids. A mobile-inspired experience designed for the precision of a desktop.
- **Dual Personality Layouts:** Seamlessly switch between a classic **Icon-Only** dock with parabolic zoom or a modern **Icon-Label** taskbar with locked-down stability. One engine, two completely different worlds.
- **Omnidirectional Mastery:** Top, Bottom, Left, or Right—Krema is axis-agnostic. Our engine transposes math dynamically, ensuring identical behavior across all screen edges.
- **Multi-Monitor & Workspace Aware:** Independent dock instances per screen, filtered by Activity and Virtual Desktop. It’s the ultimate command center for power users.
- **Fluid, Kinetic Interface:** Experience a zoom-engine built on C++23 physics. It’s high-fidelity motion that responds to your cursor with zero-latency.

## 2. Identity & Positioning
- **The Spiritual Successor:** Krema honors the legacy of iconic docks but breaks free from the architectural debt of the past. We are not a fork; we are a fresh, high-performance evolution for the modern Wayland era.
- **The Cross-Wayland Native:** Whether you use KDE Plasma or Hyprland, Krema provides the same high-performance, consistent dock experience. No more choosing between your favorite compositor and a great dock.

## 3. Communication Standards
- **Tone:** Professional, technical, and transparent. Avoid feature-comparison wars; focus purely on our own architectural advantages.
- **SEO Keywords:** "KDE Plasma 6 dock," "Hyprland dock," "Wayland-native dock," "performant dock for Plasma and Hyprland."
- **Transparency:** We advocate for our decisions (Wayland-native, math-first) based on technical data, not hype.

## 4. Public Documentation Guidelines
- **Value Prop First:** All landing pages and READMEs MUST lead with the Value Proposition (Performance + Mathematical Consistency).
- **Changelogs:** Human-readable, emphasizing user-facing impact over internal refactoring.
- **Installation:** Prioritize distro/official repos first, followed by clear, reproducible local build instructions.
